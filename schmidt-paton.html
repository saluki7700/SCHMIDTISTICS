The Schmidt–Paton Family

Where It All Started

Kenneth Oswald Schmidt (1905–1996) and Nellie Florence Bowland (1904–1991) married about 6 November 1925, in Washington County, Kansas — the marriage announced in that week's Wetmore Spectator. Everything on this page descends from that household: their children, the family that grew from them, and the reunions that have kept it together ever since.

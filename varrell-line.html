The Varrell Line

Direct line only — the confirmed pedigree chain from the family database; full source synthesis not yet written up

Sarah Varrell's own family, Isles of Shoals and York County, Maine — she married Edward Voudy, joining the Voudy Line that eventually reaches the Dudleys and the Schmidts. As with Voudy, the archive holds a large, still-being-adjudicated research record for this surname; this page shows only the direct-line ancestors already settled in the pedigree database, not a full synthesis.

The confirmed direct-line chain from John Overall Varrel Jr down to Kenneth Oswald Schmidt.

John Overall Varrel Jr & Rachel Sadler

Direct Line

Sarah Varrell

b. 14 Sep 1740 – d. 29 Dec 1811 m. Edward Voudy

John and Rachel's daughter, Sarah, married Edward Voudy — their line continues on the Voudy Line. Sarah's brother, Solomon Varrell, is a documented sibling in the pedigree database; he does not yet have his own entry on this page.

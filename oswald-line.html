Ancestor Records
001
Married into Schmidt Line

Margaret "Madge" Oswald Schmidt

A Hanover, Kansas, Native
b. 28 Oct 1883, Hanover, KS d. 1956, Encinitas, CA m. William Edward Schmidt, 1903

Madge was born in 1883 in Hanover, Kansas, the thirteenth of Mathias Oswald and Elmira Lillibridge's large family. She married William Edward Schmidt in 1903 in Washington, Kansas, and the couple raised their children — among them Kenneth Oswald Schmidt — before relocating to San Diego County, California, in the late 1920s. She died in December 1956 and is buried with William Edward at Fort Rosecrans National Cemetery, San Diego.

Bloodline — Booker

The Booker Line

The Booker family of York County, Maine — Aaron Booker, a Revolutionary War patriot, and his descendant Dorcas Booker. Through Dorcas, the Booker line runs forward into the Voudy family and on into the Dudley line and Mary Catherine Dudley, whose own ancestry is on the Dudley Line. This page follows the Bookers' own branch.

Name Spellings in This Line
Booker · Bowker · Bocker · Bucker

Largely stable as a surname, with an occasional “Bowker” turning up in deed and probate indexes. Two further spellings matter less as family variants than as search traps: the printed federal Census of Pensioners of 1841 (Maine, p. 4, York County) sets the name as “Bocker” — one o, then a c — which is why a surname search for “Booker” misses that page; and the printed Boston Births 1700–1800 (24th Report of the Boston Record Commissioners, 1894, p. 154) records “Mary daughter of William and Mary Bucker, 24 January 1722,” indexed by the volume’s own compilers as “Bucker.” Neither is a spelling the family itself used — but the second is consequential: a search of that 397-page volume for “Booker” returns zero hits, while “Bucker” returns the family. A Booker search of that particular book is a false negative, not an absence of record.

Open Questions & Discrepancies
Dorcas Booker's Birth Year

The candidates are c. 1780 (DAR application) and c. 1786 (census/FamilySearch); the c. 1796 figure has been ruled out — it would make her about seven at her 1803 marriage. The c. 1780 date is currently treated as most likely — though the family pedigree database currently carries c. 1786 for this field, which hasn't been reconciled with this page's own conclusion yet.

Aaron Booker's Own Tier

The pedigree database currently carries Aaron Booker himself at tier "open" rather than "confirmed," alongside the dashed "unproven link" already shown between him and Dorcas in the tree above. This page's own text calls him confirmed; which framing is current hasn't been reconciled yet.

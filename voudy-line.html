The Voudy Line

Direct line only — the confirmed pedigree chain from the family database; full source synthesis not yet written up

Lydia Jane Voudy's own ancestry, York County, Maine — the family that married into the Dudleys when Lydia Jane married John H. Dudley. Her mother, Sarah Varrell, has her own family on the Varrell Line. Ancestor Records
001
Direct Line

Mathew Voudy & Lydia

Mathew: b. 1705, Isles of Shoals

The earliest Voudy ancestor currently in the pedigree database — a Huguenot-descended family on the Isles of Shoals, the island fishing settlement off York and Kittery where the Voudy, Varrell, Booker, Ellingwood, and Sadler families all have roots. The surname is a research challenge in its own right: records spell it almost every way a clerk could hear it — Voudy, Voudey, Vowdy, Vowdey, Vondy, Vodery, Voodry, Vaudy — and the full-text search that finally reopened this line only returned results under the “Vondy” spelling. No death date is yet recorded for Mathew, and neither his own origins nor his wife Lydia’s are traced further back; the line is a documented wall at this generation.

002
Direct Line

Edward Voudy & Sarah Varrell

Edward: 7 Jan 1738 – abt 24 Apr 1819 Sarah: 14 Sep 1740 – 29 Dec 1811

Mathew and Lydia’s son Edward married Sarah (“Sally”) Varrell, daughter of John Varrel and Rachel Sadler of the Isles of Shoals — her own family is covered on the Varrell Line. Their marriage is recorded at the Isles themselves: “Dec’r 1st 1760. Edward Voudey & Sarah Varrell were Joyned in Marriage” (New England Historical and Genealogical Register, vol. 66, p. 146). Edward is styled a fisherman in an 1798 deed of gift from Sarah’s widowed mother, and a York overseers-of-the-poor disbursement from 1812 shows him paid for “coffathing” — coffin-making and laying-out — suggesting he also did carpentry and informal undertaking work in the community. On 10 May 1803 he conveyed land to their son William, with Sarah joining the deed to release her dower right. Edward died at York on 24 April 1819, per the First Parish necrology; Sarah had died eight years earlier, on 29 December 1811.

003
Tier: Open

William Voudy & Dorcas Booker

William: b. abt 1778 m. intention 6 Oct 1803, York, Maine

Edward and Sarah’s son William married Dorcas Booker — daughter of Aaron Booker, whose own line is covered on the Booker Line (which itself flags an unproven link one generation further up). The marriage itself is solidly documented: the York town clerk’s record reads “William Voudy and Dorcas Booker both of York intend marriage entered Octo. 6th 1803,” confirmed against two independently produced published transcripts — 1934 and 1962 — that agree word for word. William and Dorcas nonetheless both carry an “open” tier in the pedigree database, not because the marriage is in doubt, but because William’s own identification as Edward and Sarah’s son (the dashed link above) is not yet fully settled, separate from the Booker page’s own flagged link to Dorcas’s parentage. William is independently documented as a mariner — called so in his own deeds, and consistent with crew-list entries for a “William Vondy” of York serving as mate of the schooner Alert in 1826 and 1828. In an 1816 deed he named his own grandmother and mother outright: the land, he wrote, was “purchased by my grandmother Rachel Varrill… and left me by will of my mother Sally Voudy,” closing the Voudy–Varrell–Sadler chain in the family’s own words, not a researcher’s reconstruction.

004
Direct Line

Lydia Jane Voudy

b. 1819 – d. 24 Sep 1894 m. John H. Dudley

William and Dorcas’s daughter, Lydia Jane, married John H. Dudley — their daughter Mary Catherine Dudley married into the Schmidt family. Lydia Jane died at Lynn, Massachusetts, on 24 September 1894; full detail on her marriage and life, including the Massachusetts death return, is on the Dudley Line.

Open Questions & Discrepancies
William Voudy & Dorcas Booker — Open Tier

Both carry an “open” tier in the pedigree database rather than “confirmed.” The marriage itself (6 Oct 1803, York) is solidly documented; what remains unsettled is William’s own identification as Edward and Sarah’s son — the dashed “unproven link” shown in the tree above.

George & Elias Voudy

A sworn 1835 York County court deposition has William Voudy Jr. naming George and Elias as his own brothers. Both would be children of William Voudy Sr. and Dorcas Booker, but neither is yet reflected in the pedigree database’s own sibling list for Lydia Jane — flagged here rather than silently added.

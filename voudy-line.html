The Voudy Line

Direct line only — the confirmed pedigree chain from the family database; full source synthesis not yet written up

Lydia Jane Voudy's own ancestry, York County, Maine — the family that married into the Dudleys when Lydia Jane married John H. Dudley. Her mother, Sarah Varrell, has her own family on the Varrell Line. The archive holds an extensive, deeply sourced research record for this surname — hundreds of documents, many still being adjudicated between confirmed and theory tiers — which this page does not yet attempt to summarize; it shows only the direct-line ancestors already settled in the pedigree database.

The confirmed direct-line chain from Mathew Voudy down to Kenneth Oswald Schmidt.
Ancestor Records
001
Direct Line

Mathew Voudy & Lydia

Mathew: b. 1705, Isles of Shoals

The earliest Voudy ancestor currently in the pedigree database, at the Isles of Shoals — the island fishing settlement off York and Kittery where this family and the allied Varrell, Booker, Ellingwood, and Sadler families all have roots. No death date is yet recorded for Mathew; his wife Lydia's own origins are not yet traced further back.

002
Direct Line

Edward Voudy & Sarah Varrell

Edward: 7 Jan 1738 – abt 24 Apr 1819 Sarah: 14 Sep 1740 – 29 Dec 1811

Mathew and Lydia's son, Edward, married Sarah Varrell — her own family is covered on the Varrell Line. Sarah's brother Solomon Varrell is a documented sibling, not yet given his own entry here.

003
Tier: Open

William Voudy & Dorcas Booker

William: b. abt 1778 m. 1803, York, Maine

Edward and Sarah's son William married Dorcas Booker — daughter of Aaron Booker, whose own line is covered on the Booker Line (which itself flags an unproven link one generation further up). Both William and Dorcas carry an "open" tier in the database, meaning their own identification is not yet fully settled, separate from the Booker page's own flagged link.

004
Direct Line

Lydia Jane Voudy

b. 1819 – d. 24 Sep 1894 m. John H. Dudley

William and Dorcas's daughter, Lydia Jane, married John H. Dudley — their daughter Mary Catherine Dudley married into the Schmidt family. Full detail on this marriage and Lydia Jane's life is on the Dudley Line.

Schmidt & Bowland Daughter

Marjorie Irene (Schmidt) Paton

Youngest Child of Kenneth and Nellie
b. Nov 26, 1928 d. Jun 8, 2022, Elgin, IL m. Frank William Paton, Feb 1, 1949, Cook County, IL

Marjorie was the youngest of Kenneth Oswald Schmidt and Nellie Florence Bowland's three children, after Richard Dean and Ronald Ray (see the Schmidt Line and the Bowland Line). She married Frank William Paton on Feb 1, 1949, in Cook County, Illinois — a date confirmed independently by the Cook County marriage index and Frank's own memorial record. She died in 2022, near Elgin, Illinois, at 93.

002
Married into Schmidt Line

Frank William Paton

b. Sep 20, 1924, Chicago, Cook Co., IL d. Dec 17, 2019, Oak Lawn, Cook Co., IL buried Chapel Hill Gardens South, Oak Lawn, IL

Frank was born in Chicago in 1924 and died in Oak Lawn, Illinois, in 2019, where he is buried at Chapel Hill Gardens South. His marriage to Marjorie and his vital dates are confirmed by his Find a Grave memorial and the Cook County marriage index; his own parents and ancestry have not yet been researched.

Frank William Paton's Parents

Parents of Frank William Paton

No research has begun on Frank's own parents or his own ancestry. His own siblings and children, however, are named in his obituary: Frank was the brother of the late John, Harold, and George Paton, and the father of Richard, James, Roni, and Cindy. His eldest son, Richard Franklin Paton (b. 12 Apr 1950), is Rich Paton — the family researcher who has separately contributed leads on the Dudley and Voudy lines, including a Voudy pedigree chart and testimony on the John H. Dudley parentage question (sources X-PATONTREE, X-GILMANTEST, X-UNCLERICH-2 and others). That relationship, previously undocumented here, was confirmed directly by Blaine in October 2026.
